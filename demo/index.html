Explain it in one minute

“I compared a TF-IDF MLP, a TextCNN with GloVe embeddings, a two-layer BiLSTM with attention, and fine-tuned DistilBERT. All used the same reviews and split. I chose learning rates and checkpoints using validation F1, then evaluated the selected models once on the test set. DistilBERT performed best, while the MLP required much less compute.”

What should I say about limitations?

The experiment uses one seed and a small tuning budget. Long reviews are truncated. DistilBERT uses subwords rather than the word-level vocabulary. Some duplicate reviews cross the split boundaries, and the custom split is not directly comparable to the official benchmark.
