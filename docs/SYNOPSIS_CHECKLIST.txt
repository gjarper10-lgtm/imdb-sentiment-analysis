Methodological clarifications:
1. DistilBERT retains its pretrained WordPiece vocabulary, not the 10,000-word
   vocabulary of the word-level models. Its 512-token limit includes special tokens.
2. GloVe is pretrained. CNN/BiLSTM are non-transformer models with pretrained word
   embeddings, not entirely from-scratch systems.
3. The raw HuggingFace dataset is used instead of combining the already-tokenized
   Keras variant with raw reviews. All models use identical review identities.
4. A shared PyTorch training loop implements the proposed fine-tuning instead of
   HuggingFace Trainer. It keeps loss/selection/timing comparable across architectures.
5. The 70/15/15 pooled split follows the synopsis but differs from the official
   Stanford test split. Scores must not be claimed as directly comparable leaderboard results.
6. Two learning rates and a limited epoch budget are a modest search, not an exhaustive one.
7. Latency excludes tokenization. All timing depends on hardware and batching.
